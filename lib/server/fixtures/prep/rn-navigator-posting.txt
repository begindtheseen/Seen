RN Patient Navigator - Cardiology

Desert Heart Institute is a cardiology practice with six clinics across the Phoenix valley.

Responsibilities
- Guide newly diagnosed heart failure patients through tests, referrals and follow-up visits
- Educate patients and families on medications, diet and warning signs
- Coordinate care with cardiologists, home health agencies and insurers
- Track patients after discharge and escalate concerns to the care team

Qualifications
- Active Arizona RN license
- 3+ years of acute care or cardiac nursing experience
- BLS required; ACLS preferred
- Experience with Epic
