Kitchen Lead

Copper Fork Bistro is a scratch kitchen in downtown Austin serving 250 covers on a busy night.

Responsibilities
- Lead the line during service and keep tickets moving
- Train and coach new cooks on recipes and station standards
- Check food quality and plating before every dish leaves the pass
- Manage prep lists, inventory and weekly food orders
- Enforce food safety and sanitation standards

Requirements
- 2+ years of line cook experience in a high-volume kitchen
- ServSafe certification
- Ability to stand for 8+ hours and lift 50 lbs
- Weekend and evening availability

Pay: $19 - $22 per hour
