Junior Data Analyst

About Harbor Health Plans
Harbor Health Plans is a nonprofit health insurer serving 400,000 members across Texas.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau that track claims and member outcomes
- Write SQL queries to pull and clean data from the claims warehouse
- Present findings to operations and clinical leaders in plain language
- Partner with the actuarial team to validate monthly reporting

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in statistics, economics, computer science or a related field
- Experience with SQL and a visualization tool such as Tableau or Power BI
- Familiarity with Python or R
- Experience with healthcare claims data is a plus

Pay: $62,000 - $70,000 per year
