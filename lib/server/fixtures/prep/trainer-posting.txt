Call Center Trainer

About us
Meridian Health Partners runs member services for regional health plans, answering 1.2 million member calls a year from our Denver contact center.

What you'll do
- Deliver new-hire training for classes of 10-15 customer service agents, from day one through nesting
- Create or improve training materials, job aids and e-learning modules
- Coach agents through live call monitoring and roleplay
- Coaching observable behaviors through call review and side-by-side sessions
- Partner with team leads and quality assurance to find skill gaps and build refresher sessions
- Track trainee progress and report on speed-to-proficiency
- Agent performance improvement through targeted coaching plans

What you bring
- 2-5 years of call center experience in sales or customer service
- 1+ year of experience training, coaching or leading agents
- Experience with a call center platform such as Talkdesk, Genesys or Five9
- Strong presentation skills and comfort facilitating groups of 15
- Proficiency with Microsoft Office and an LMS

Pay: $24.00 - $28.00 per hour
Schedule: Monday-Friday, 8am-5pm, on-site in Denver
