Apprentice Lineman/Groundman
About Us:
We are an automation and electrical integrator providing services to the oil and gas, industrial, and related industries. Our expertise covers virtually all facets of electrical contracting for the upstream oil and gas industries, including specialty expertise in new well sites and production facility electrification.
Job Summary:
Apprentice Linemen/Groundsman works with line crews in the power/utility industry supporting linemen who install and maintain electrical distribution and transmission lines.
Essential Duties & Responsibilities:
Set up job sites and safety zones
Hand tools and materials to lineman
Dig holes, set poles and anchor guy wires
Operate equipment augers, trenchers, bucket trucks
Once trained direct traffic in work zones
Maintain and organize tools and trucks
Qualifications:
Basic understanding of electricity and safety
Ability to follow strict safety protocols
Team communication - working closely with a crew
Class C or Class A Driver's License; CDL preferred
Physical Demands:
Heavy lifting 50-100lbs
Climbing
Bending
Working at heights
Working outdoors in all weather (Texas heat included)
Work Environment:
 • Outdoor job sites such as utility right-of-way work, construction yards, or maintenance sites.
 • Variable conditions (traffic control zones, slopes, wet/ice surfaces, uneven ground).
