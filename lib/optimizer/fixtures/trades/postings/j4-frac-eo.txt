Frac Equipment Operator
Frac Equipment Operators are responsible for the following:
* Onsite rig up and rig down
* Running equipment during frac operations
* Maintaining equipment while on frac locations and at the yard
* Performing pump maintenance when required
* Maintain iron and other frac items
* Driving equipment from job to job (Class A CDL Required)
* Working safely at all times by following all safety protocols
* Job is 14 days on 7 days off work rotation
* The above responsibilities are the primary tasks for this position; however, the position is NOT limited by these duties. *
Job Type: Full-time
Pay: $16.00 - $24.00 per hour
Benefits:
* 401(k)
* Dental insurance
* Health insurance
Work Location: On the road
