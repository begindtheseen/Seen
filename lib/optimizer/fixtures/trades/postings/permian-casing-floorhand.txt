Casing Floorhand
Job Overview
Permian Basin Staffing is looking to hire a Casing Floorhand . ! In this role, you will assist with the installation, maintenance, and operation of casing on oil rigs, ensuring safe and efficient drilling operations. Your hands-on work will contribute directly to the success of drilling projects, supporting crew members and maintaining strict safety standards. This paid position offers an exciting opportunity to develop your skills in heavy equipment operation, rigging, and pipeline management while working outdoors in a fast-paced environment. If you're eager to learn, physically fit, and passionate about safety and teamwork, this role is your gateway to a rewarding career in energy production.
Responsibilities
* Assist with the handling and positioning of casing pipes during drilling operations using heavy equipment and rigging tools
* Support the crew in maintaining proper pipe connections, ensuring alignment and secure fastening according to safety protocols
* Operate pumping systems to facilitate fluid circulation and manage pressure during casing installation
* Conduct equipment troubleshooting to identify mechanical issues promptly and assist with repairs or adjustments as needed
* Follow all safety procedures including PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) use, respiratory protection, and OSHA (Occupational Safety and Health Administration) standards
* Manage outdoor work tasks efficiently while adhering to construction site safety protocols and environmental guidelines
* Collaborate with crew members to ensure smooth workflow on the rig floor, including managing pipeline connections and horizontal drilling components
Requirements
* Prior experience in oil & gas operations or construction sites is preferred but not mandatory; willingness to learn is essential
* Knowledge of pipeline handling, rigging, heavy equipment operation, and pumping systems is highly desirable
* Mechanical aptitude with the ability to troubleshoot equipment issues quickly and effectively
* Demonstrated outdoor work experience in challenging environments such as oil rigs or construction sites
* Strong understanding of safety protocols including OSHA regulations, PPE use, respiratory protection, and hazard awareness
* Ability to operate heavy machinery safely and efficiently in outdoor settings involving horizontal drilling or other complex operations
* Excellent teamwork skills with a proactive attitude toward crew management and safety compliance
Join us in powering progress through safe, efficient energy solutions! This paid position offers hands-on experience in a thriving industry where your efforts directly contribute to energy development worldwide. We value safety-minded individuals eager to grow their skills while working outdoors on challenging projects.
