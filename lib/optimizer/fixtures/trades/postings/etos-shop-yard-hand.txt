Shop Yard Hand
Applicant must have a valid Driver's License and must be over the age of 24 due to company insurance requirements. The primary role of the Shop Yard Hand is to maintain the shop and yard, work in the wash bay, and working on equipment. Duties will include but not limited to picking up trash, mowing, pressure washing equipment, receiving in equipment, and assessments of equipment, working to fix equipment to get it back on the ready line. You will also unload any deliveries that come into the shop and use a forklift, skid steer, and telehandler frequently. Will fill out proper paperwork and take pictures and videos of work and upload to app. Must read, write and speak English. Work will be performed at the shop. Must be local as no Housing is provided. Will require weekend, on-call, and after hours work. Must have own reliable transportation to and from work daily. Must be able to meet background requirements and pass a drug and alcohol test regularly. Shift is a 10 on and 4 off schedule. Be able to work independently and stay on task. Willing to learn.
