Summary The
CRM &
Lifecycle Marketing Coordinator will keep Topstep's CRM instance clean, organized, and running smoothly. This role owns the day-to-day operational health of the CRM: database hygiene, subscription management, folder and file organization, campaign setup and tracking, and QA, and will provide hands-on execution support for lifecycle and evergreen email programs.
The ideal candidate is highly organized, detail-obsessed, and comfortable owning recurring, process-driven work. You're curious about marketing technology and enjoy exploring and standing up new tools, and you take pride in a well-maintained system that others can rely on. You will work closely with the Sr.
Lifecycle & CRM Marketing Lead and collaborate with Creative and QA partners to keep lifecycle communications running without a hitch Key
Responsibilities CRM Platform & Database Management Keep contact lists clean and healthy: merge duplicate records, process unsubscribes/unbounce requests, and audit subscription and preference center settings to ensure they work as intended. Maintain the folder structures and naming conventions for content, creative, and file assets within HubSpot so teams can find what they need.

Campaign & Lifecycle Execution Support Support lifecycle and evergreen email programs by monitoring for breaks or errors, tracking send volume, and flagging opportunities for improvement.
Set up and configure campaigns in the CRM to ensure proper tracking and attribution of email performance. Partner with QA teams to run pre-send testing (rendering, links, personalization tokens, deliverability) across lifecycle and campaign emails. Cross-Functional Collaboration Work closely with Engineering, Marketing Operations and Analytics teams.
Coordinate with Creative and Brand partners on asset intake, organization, and file handoffs. Support QA and testing partners to ensure every send meets quality standards before launch. Required Qualifications and Key Competencies 3+ years of experience in email marketing operations, CRM administration, or marketing operations.
Highly detail-oriented and organized, with a strong sense of ownership over recurring, process-driven work. Experience with database hygiene practices such as deduplication, list segmentation, and subscription/preference management. Familiarity with QA and testing processes for email campaigns. Basic email building and scheduling experience.
Experience in Google slides for process document creation. Must have basic HTML knowledge (CSS and Handlebar (Or equivalent) is a plus). Curiosity and comfort exploring and adopting new marketing technology and tools.
