Summary of Position: 

 The Equipment Operator supports safe and efficient frac operations by operating and maintaining equipment, assisting with rig-up and rig-down, and ensuring equipment is ready for each stage.
  • Perform required pre-trip and post-trip inspections.
  • Operate trucks, forklifts, and frac equipment.
  • Assist with rig-up and rig-down of high- and low-pressure equipment.
  • Perform basic maintenance on trucks, pumping equipment, frac iron, and hoses.
  • Maintain required DOT and equipment inspection documentation.
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and safe work area.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Valid Class A CDL with HazMat endorsement.
  • Strong communication skills.
  • Ability to work safely and effectively in a team environment.
  • Ability to think and respond quickly in changing conditions.
  • Willingness to learn and take on additional responsibilities.

What We Look For

  • Safety-focused mindset.
  • Dependability and accountability.
  • Strong attention to detail.
  • Positive attitude and willingness to learn.
  • Ability to prioritize and follow through.
  • Team-oriented approach.
  • Strong customer focus and professional conduct.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Ensure assigned equipment is inspected and ready to operate on time.
  • Check fluids and visually inspect equipment before startup.
  • Safely and efficiently rig up and rig down frac equipment.
  • Operate assigned frac equipment during operations.
  • Remain attentive to assigned equipment and promptly identify potential issues.
  • Communicate equipment status and concerns to the Frac Supervisor.
  • Perform basic preventive maintenance as required.
  • Follow all Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures.
  • Maintain packing lubrication and other equipment service requirements.
  • Complete required equipment inspections and documentation.
  • Verify equipment is properly shut down and secured.
  • Assist with housekeeping and maintaining a clean location.

Other duties and responsibilities may be assigned based on operational needs.

Working Conditions

  • Work is performed in both indoor and outdoor environments.
  • Regular exposure to dust, odors, oil, fumes, noise, and changing weather conditions.
  • Must consistently follow established safety requirements and procedures.
