Summary of Position:

The LPI Project Manager will lead the planning, execution, and delivery of natural gas-based power plant construction projects. The Project Manager will oversee the complete lifecycle—from design and permitting to construction, commissioning, and handover to Operations - ensuring the project is delivered on time, within budget, and in compliance with all technical, commercial, and regulatory requirements. This role requires deep expertise in gas power plant technologies, EPC contract management, and multidisciplinary project coordination.

Qualifications & Requirements:
  • Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, or Energy Engineering.
  • Master’s degree in Engineering, Project Management, or Business (preferred).
  • PMP Certification (preferred).
  • Minimum 3 years experience managing large-scale energy, infrastructure, industrial, or construction projects preferred.
  • Minimum 3 years of experience managing natural gas power plant construction.
  • Strong understanding of reciprocating engine and/or open cycle gas turbine (“OCGT”) technologies and auxiliary systems.
  • Strong knowledge of project management methodologies, contracts (EPC, EPCM, lump-sum, turnkey), and regulatory frameworks.
  • Expertise in EPC project management, construction sequencing, and contractor coordination.
  • Proficient in project management tools (Primavera P6, MS Project).
  • Familiar with industry codes and standards (ASME, API, NFPA, etc.).
  • Knowledge of permitting and grid interconnection processes.
  • PMP (Project Management Professional) preferred.
  • OSHA/HSE Certification preferred.
  • Professional Engineer (PE) license preferred
Indicators & Attributes for Success:
  • Strong leadership and negotiation skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication.
  • Strong problem-solving ability and high attention to detail.
  • Effective and decisive decision-making skills.
  • Strong organizational and prioritization skills.
  • Persuasive customer/client focus
Primary Responsibilities:
  • Develop detailed project execution plans, including scope, schedule, and deliverables.
  • Lead feasibility assessments, site evaluations, and permitting processes.
  • Define project objectives aligned with strategic and regulatory goals.
  • Manage Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) contractors and sub-contractors.
  • Oversee construction activities, ensuring adherence to design specifications, safety standards, and quality benchmarks.
  • Coordinate procurement and logistics for major equipment such as gas reciprocating engines/packaged engine modules and balance-of-plant systems.
  • Prepare and manage project budgets, forecasts, and cost control mechanisms.
  • Monitor expenditures and approve project-related financial transactions.
  • Report regularly on budget performance, identifying and addressing variances.
  • Act as the primary liaison between LPI, customers, consultants, contractors, local authorities, and utility providers.
  • Lead regular project review meetings and ensure timely communication of progress, risks, and decisions.
  • Manage community engagement and support compliance with environmental and zoning regulations.
  • Identify project risks and develop mitigation plans.
  • Ensure compliance with environmental, health & safety (EHS), and regulatory requirements.
  • Conduct project audits and quality assurance reviews throughout all phases.
  • Oversee pre-commissioning and commissioning activities.
  • Ensure all systems meet performance criteria before handover.
  • Manage documentation, training, and transition to operations/asset management teams

Working Conditions/Environment:
  • Role involves both office and on-site presence.
  • Travel to construction site(s) and stakeholder meetings required.
  • May involve extended working hours during peak construction and commissioning phases.
  • Routinely uses standard office equipment such as laptops, photocopiers/scanners, and smart phones.
