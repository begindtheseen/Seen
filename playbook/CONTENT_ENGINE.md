# The Content Engine — 52 shots a year

## 1. The Weekly Ghost Report (the flagship, 30 min/week)

Auto-generated from the DB (admin tool builds the page + share image). Your job: post it.

**Where:** r/recruitinghell (check self-promo rules; if strict, post the DATA as text/image
with the source in comments), LinkedIn (surprisingly strong for hiring-outrage content),
X/Threads. Rotate one new venue per month (r/jobs, r/antiwork adjacent, Hacker News when
there's a genuinely interesting data angle).

**Title formulas that fit the data we actually have (never inflate):**
- "We tracked which staffing agencies respond to applicants. Week [N]: [X] of [Y] reported applications got ANY response."
- "[Agency] has now been reported for ghosting [N] times. Here's the running index."
- "The average reported wait before a company goes silent: [N] days. Data from [N] applicant reports."
- Honest-small version (use it while N is small — it WORKS): "I'm building a public ghost-rate index. [N] reports in. Here's what it shows so far — and why I need yours."

**Anatomy of the post:** the image/table first · one-paragraph plain-English finding ·
methodology in one sentence ("applicant-reported outcomes, trust-weighted; methodology
public") · the ask ("been ghosted? add it to the record — 2 minutes") · founder
disclosure. Reply to every comment for the first 2 hours (the algorithm and the humans
both reward it).

## 2. Demo clips (2/week, 30–45 min each, screen-record + voiceover)

The V2 résumé engine's panels are made for this. 20–40 seconds, vertical, captions on.

**Clip script A — "The transferable-proof reveal":**
Screen: paste a valet/retail/warehouse résumé + a customer-service job posting → run it.
VO: "This valet thinks he's unqualified for this customer service job. Watch. …The engine
finds the proof in the résumé he didn't know was there — guest service, time pressure,
cash handling — and shows exactly why each one counts. And it never invents anything —
every claim is traced to a line in the résumé." End card: seenjobs.io.

**Clip script B — "Check the ghost rate BEFORE you apply":**
Screen: search a job → open company/agency page → the grade + 'no data yet, be the first'
honesty. VO: "Before you spend an hour on this application: does this company even respond
to people? …That's a real number from real applicants, not a vibe. And when there's no
data, it says so — no fake scores." End: "Check yours before you apply."

**Clip script C — "The outcome card" (once shares ship):**
Screen: mark an application ghosted → generate the card → share. VO: "They ghosted me
after three interviews. So I put it on the record." (This one is the virality bet — test
weekly variants of the emotional read.)

**Formula rotation:** A and B weekly until analytics shows a winner; then double down.

## 3. What NOT to do
- No engagement-bait fake stats. One caught fabrication kills the whole thesis.
- No posting the same link to 5 subs in one day (spam filters + community memory).
- No arguing with hostile comments; answer the honest ones, upvote-and-leave the rest.
- No skipping the founder disclosure. It's the moat nobody can copy: a real person
  keeping receipts in public.

## 4. The monthly retro (first Monday of each month, part of the weekly brief)
Which titles/venues/clips produced actual sessions (PostHog referrers)? Kill the bottom
half of formats, double the top. The content engine is itself subject to the kill-gates.
