# Sprint 1 — The First 90 Days

> Day 0 = the day the Wave-1 PRs merge. Every gate is a number in the DB or PostHog —
> never a vibe. Gates get checked in the Monday brief.

## Phase 1 — Weeks 1–2: Infrastructure of sight & sale (mostly Claude's build work)

**Ships:** PostHog analytics · share instrumentation · 7-day no-card trial · one-time
SKUs (Interview Sprint $14.99, Credit Pack $4.99) · Staffing Agency Ghost Index (/agencies)
· niche landing (/ghosted) · one-tap report path · import labeling · outcome email loop
(Resend) · weekly Ghost Report generator.

**Owner (once):** PostHog account + key in Vercel · Resend account + domain DNS + key ·
approve/apply the pro_until migration · confirm Stripe webhook events.

**Owner (rhythm starts NOW, product ready or not):** 15 min/day community presence.
Distribution starts before the product is "ready" — the presence is the seed.

## Phase 2 — Weeks 3–5: The beachhead opens

- First weekly Ghost Report posted (use the honest-small title formula).
- DM seeding begins: 10–15/week toward **50 real reports by day ~60**.
- First 2 demo clips posted; venue/format log started.
- Employer outreach begins: 10/week off the generated lists (badge pitch to good scorers
  first — easier first yes than the agency program).

## Phase 3 — Weeks 6–12: The loop runs and the numbers talk

- Full weekly cadence (see WEEKLY_ROUTINE.md definition of done).
- Claude iterates weekly on whatever analytics exposes: landing conversion, trial funnel
  drop-off, which content formats produce sessions, report-flow abandonment.
- Monthly retro at weeks 6 and 10: kill bottom-half content formats, double winners.

## Day-90 gates (kill / scale decisions — enforced, not renegotiated)

| Gate | Number | If HIT | If MISSED |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1: Atomic network forming | ≥50 organic reports from ≥30 distinct users | Scale E1: widen from agencies to one more niche | Diagnose seeding mechanics (friction? audience? ask?) — fix and re-run 30 days; do NOT widen |
| G2: Amplifier works | ≥1 post >10k impressions OR >500 sessions from content | Double posting volume; test paid boost $50 | Rotate venues/formats; if missed again at day 180 → content demoted to 1 post/wk |
| G3: E2 funnel real | ≥25 trials started | Optimize conversion (trial→paid), consider card-required test | Trial isn't the constraint — traffic is; fold effort into G1/G2 |
| G4: E3 alive | ≥5 one-time purchases | Feature Sprint pack harder at desperation moments | Reprice/reposition once; twice missed → park SKUs |
| G5: E4 alive | ≥3 employer replies OR 1 paid listing/badge | Systematize: 20 emails/wk, build self-serve checkout page | Change the pitch target (agencies↔employers) once; twice missed → park until data grows |
| G6: FIRST DOLLAR | ≥$1 real revenue from ANY engine by day 90 | Celebrate loudly (post about it — it's content) | Allowed miss ONLY if G1+G2 both hit (data+attention precede money); else full plan review |

## The reallocation rule
At each 90-day boundary: rank engines by (evidence of life ÷ effort spent). Top engine
gets ~70% of the next sprint's effort. Nothing gets a third sprint on zero evidence.

## Month 6 / 12 markers (from the approved plan)
- M6: $200–500 MRR-equivalent · 200+ organic reports · one reliable content format
- M12: first $1k month · winning engine identified and fed
- Insurance pivot (pre-decided): if all engines gate out twice → employer-side data
  product on the outcome graph (the levels.fyi B2B path). The dataset survives any
  consumer-side failure.
