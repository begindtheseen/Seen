# Outreach Scripts — copy, personalize the [brackets], send

## A. Report-seeding DMs (E1) — to people who PUBLICLY posted a ghosting story

Target: authors of fresh posts/comments on r/recruitinghell, r/jobs, r/jobsearchhacks
describing being ghosted (especially by staffing agencies). One DM, no follow-up.

**Script 1 — the direct ask (default):**
> Hey — saw your post about [company/agency] going silent after [detail from their post].
> That genuinely sucks, and it's exactly why I built seenjobs.io — I'm tracking which
> companies and staffing agencies ghost applicants, in public, so the next person can
> check before they waste weeks. If you're up for it, reporting what happened takes about
> two minutes: [link — /report?company=X&outcome=ghosted]. I'm the founder, not a bot,
> happy to answer anything.

**Script 2 — agency-specific (when a staffing agency did it):**
> Saw your post about [agency]. Staffing agencies are statistically the worst ghosting
> offenders (~2/3 of agency applications get no response in the research I've seen), and
> nobody tracks them by name — so I started an index that does: seenjobs.io/agencies.
> If you add what happened to you it becomes part of [agency]'s public record. Two
> minutes, anonymous if you want: [prefilled link]. Founder here, building this in the open.

**Script 3 — the soft version (for subs/threads where links feel pushy):**
> This happened to me enough times that I started keeping score publicly — there's now a
> ghost-rate index for staffing agencies. Not going to drop a link uninvited, but if you
> want to add [agency] to the record, DM me or search seenjobs.

**Volume + hygiene:** 10–15/week max. Personalize the first line EVERY time (quote their
actual situation). Never DM someone who didn't publicly describe being ghosted. If Reddit
mutes/warns, stop for a week and shift volume to comments-with-value.

## B. Employer / agency outreach (E4) — 10 emails/week

Weekly lists are generated for you (companies with good scores → badge pitch; agencies
with bad signals → response-program pitch; recent job posters → featured listing pitch).

**Template 1 — "Transparency Verified" badge (companies that already look good):**
Subject: [Company] is one of the few that actually responds to applicants
> Hi [name] — I run SeenJobs, a hiring-transparency site where job seekers check
> companies' response records before applying. Here's the part you'll like: your data
> looks GOOD. [Company]'s reported response behavior is better than most of your industry.
> We're launching a "Transparency Verified" badge for employers who commit to responding
> to applicants — verified against real applicant-reported outcomes, not self-attestation.
> It's [$X/mo], it goes on your company page and listings, and candidates are actively
> filtering for it. Want the one-pager?

**Template 2 — response program (agencies with poor signals):**
Subject: [Agency]'s applicant response record — before it spreads further
> Hi [name] — I run SeenJobs. We publish a Staffing Agency Ghost Index based on
> applicant-reported outcomes, and [agency] currently doesn't look great on it. I'm not
> writing to shake you down — the index reflects whatever applicants report, good or bad.
> I'm writing because agencies that commit to our response program (respond to every
> applicant within 14 days; we verify against reported outcomes) get the improvement
> reflected publicly, with a verified badge. If applicant experience is something you're
> working on, this is the receipts. 15 minutes this week?

**Template 3 — featured listing (anyone actively hiring in the niche):**
Subject: Put [Company]'s opening in front of people vetting employers
> Hi [name] — SeenJobs is where job seekers check whether an employer actually responds
> to applications before they apply. Featured listings sit at the top of those searches
> with a "responds to applicants" panel. It's [$X] flat for 30 days. Interested?

**Rules:** truthful always — never threaten, never imply we'd alter data for money (we
won't and can't; scores are computed from reports). The badge/program sells VERIFIED
improvement, not reputation laundering. If asked, the methodology page and legal audit
export exist precisely to show the receipts.

## C. When someone accuses you of running a shakedown (it will happen once)

> Fair question. The index is computed from applicant reports and public signals — money
> doesn't change a score, and there's a public methodology page plus a signed audit trail
> proving it. The paid program is a commitment to respond to applicants going forward,
> verified the same way the bad news was. The data stays honest either way.
